# Product Principles

## 1. One Operation

All work should converge into one operating system for content and social activity.

Users should not feel they are moving between separate products for creation, automation, publishing, analytics, and optimization.

## 2. Shared Context

Pages, connections, content, media, templates, automations, publications, interactions, and analytics must share product context.

A page should be recognizable as the same page across publishing, automation, analytics, and settings.

## 3. Create Once, Distribute Many

Content can be produced once and adapted into variants, outputs, publications, and distribution targets.

The product model should support reuse without forcing duplicate manual work.

## 4. Automation as Infrastructure

Automation is not an isolated feature.

It is an operational layer that can connect social events, conditions, actions, content, messages, waits, branches, executions, and outcomes.

## 5. Data Closes the Loop

Publishing does not end the journey.

Metrics, interactions, and operational activity must feed analytics and optimization so users can improve future content, automations, and distribution choices.

## 6. AI as a Layer

AI / Intelligence is not a single "AI button" or isolated chatbot.

It should eventually support generation, analysis, recommendation, optimization, classification, summarization, assistance, and transformation across product domains.

## 7. Desktop and Mobile Are Different Experiences

Desktop is productivity-first.

Mobile WebApp is app-first and touch-first.

They share domains, backend concepts, identity, foundations, and language, but they may differ in flows, density, navigation, and components.

## Anti-goals

Creatye Studio must not become:

- A collection of disconnected tools.
- A clone of Manychat.
- A clone of CapCut.
- A clone of mLabs.
- A generic CMS.
- A generic CRM.
- A standalone video editor.
- A standalone analytics panel.
- AI added only as a chatbot.
- A product where mobile is just compressed desktop.
