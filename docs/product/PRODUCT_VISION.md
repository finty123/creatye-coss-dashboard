# Product Vision

## What is Creatye Studio?

Creatye Studio is an integrated operating system for content and social operations.

It brings creation, automation, publishing, distribution, analytics, intelligence, and optimization into one product model so teams can run a content operation without switching mental models between disconnected tools.

## Who is it for?

Creatye Studio is for teams and operators who manage social pages, content production, publishing workflows, automation, and performance improvement across multiple channels.

Primary users include workspace owners, admins, editors, operators, and future AI-assisted agents working on behalf of the operation.

## What operation does it centralize?

Creatye Studio centralizes the loop:

CREATE -> AUTOMATE -> PUBLISH / DISTRIBUTE -> ANALYZE -> OPTIMIZE -> REPEAT

The product should make the operation feel continuous: content, pages, automations, publications, interactions, and metrics all belong to one shared operating context.

## What makes it integrated?

Creatye Studio is integrated when:

- Social pages and connections are reusable across creation, automation, publishing, and analytics.
- Content can move from draft to variants, outputs, publications, metrics, and optimization without becoming a separate artifact at each step.
- Automations can react to social events and content activity.
- Analytics can explain performance across content, pages, publications, automations, and operations.
- AI can assist across domains rather than appearing as a separate chatbot product.

## What is outside the current product vision?

Creatye Studio is not:

- A generic CMS.
- A generic CRM.
- A standalone video editor.
- A standalone image editor.
- A standalone analytics dashboard.
- A clone of any existing social automation, publishing, or creative editing product.
- A disconnected bundle of tools.

The product may include capabilities found in those categories, but they must serve the shared content and social operation.
