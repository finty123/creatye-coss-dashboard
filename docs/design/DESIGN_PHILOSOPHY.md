# Design Philosophy

Creatye Studio is a professional operating platform for content and social operations. It contains creative tools, but its primary interface is operational software.

## Character

The product should transmit:

- Professional
- Precise
- Modern
- Controlled
- Capable
- Fast
- Premium
- Operational

The product should not transmit:

- Playful startup
- Futuristic neon
- Crypto product
- Gaming UI
- Generic AI app
- Marketing landing page
- Childish
- Overdesigned

## Reference philosophy

References are used to study quality, not to copy identity.

Useful reference qualities:

- Structural clarity of professional tools.
- Consistency of mature design systems.
- Efficiency of creative tools.
- Appropriate density for operational software.
- Precise hierarchy and editorial-grade layout.

Creatye Studio must not become a visual clone of Figma, Linear, GitHub, Atlassian, Framer, Adobe, Canva, or any other product.

## Anti-generic rules

Avoid by default:

- Glassmorphism.
- Decorative gradients.
- Glow and neon.
- Decorative blobs.
- Excess blur.
- Giant cards.
- Exaggerated radius.
- Exaggerated shadows.
- Giant dashboard numbers as decoration.
- Excess whitespace.
- Every section inside a card.
- Purple AI buttons with sparkle as the default AI treatment.
- Decorative components without function.
- Generic SaaS landing page aesthetics.
