# Shape and Elevation

## Radius

Use a restrained radius scale:

- `radius.0` - square / none.
- `radius.1` - small controls and dense table elements.
- `radius.2` - default controls and compact surfaces.
- `radius.3` - larger surfaces when needed.
- `radius.full` - pills, avatars, and circular controls only.

Avoid exaggerated radius as a default brand device.

## Borders and dividers

Use borders and dividers for structure:

- Separate dense rows.
- Define panel boundaries.
- Clarify nested regions.
- Support low-elevation grouping.

Prefer subtle dividers before decorative shadows.

## Containers and cards

Containers are structural regions. Cards are a specialized container used when grouping, elevation, interaction, or separation is needed.

Do not wrap every section in a card.

## Elevation

Use a small elevation hierarchy:

- Base: normal surfaces.
- Raised: popovers, menus, compact floating controls.
- Overlay: dialogs, command surfaces, blocking panels.
- Modal: highest attention, limited use.

Elevation should clarify stacking and interaction, not decorate.
