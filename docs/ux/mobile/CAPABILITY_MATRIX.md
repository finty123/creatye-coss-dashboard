## Interpretation

- FULL means complete platform capability.
- SIMPLIFIED means reduced capability optimized for platform context.
- QUICK CREATE means fast creation flow, not full studio parity.
- MONITOR / LIMITED means observe, inspect, and act lightly, not deeply configure.
- AGENDA means operational schedule view, not complex monthly planning.
- SUMMARY means decision-oriented overview, not deep exploration.
