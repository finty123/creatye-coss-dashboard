15. Analytics Summary.
16. Notifications.
17. Profile / Settings.

## Rules

- Tabs do not become independent screens automatically.
- Sheets do not become independent screens automatically.
- Dialogs do not become independent screens automatically.
- States do not become independent screens automatically.
- Pages, Automations, Analytics, Settings, and AI do not become bottom-nav items by default.
