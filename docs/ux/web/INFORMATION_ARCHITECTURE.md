# Information Architecture

## Principle

The Web Desktop IA is organized around the approved operational areas, not around every capability as a sidebar item.

Capabilities remain connected through context and workflows.

## Primary areas

| Area | Product role | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Home | Operational command center. | Not a promotional dashboard. |
| Pages | Social page management and page-scoped context. | Page can preserve context into Content, Publishing, Automations, and Analytics. |
| Content | Parent area for creation and material management. | Contains Library, Video Studio, Image Studio, Templates. |
| Automations | Automation management and flow work. | Contains list, templates, activity, analytics, and detail/flow builder. |
| Publishing | Integrated publishing operation. | Calendar and List are views, not separate products. |
| Analytics | Actionable operational analytics. | Leads back to operational actions. |
| Settings | Global configuration. | Workspace, members, connections, preferences, notifications, security, platform/integration configuration. |

## Transversal concepts

- AI / Intelligence.
- Search / Command.
- Notifications.
- Workspace Context.
- Page Context.
- Global Create.

Transversal concepts do not automatically become primary navigation areas.

## Approved nesting

Content is the parent for:

- Library.
- Video Studio.
- Image Studio.
- Templates.

Publishing is one integrated area:

- Calendar View.
- List View.

Analytics is grouped by analysis perspective:

- Overview.
- Content.
- Pages.
- Automations.
- Publishing.

## Guardrails

- Do not add Video Studio, Image Studio, Templates, Calendar, Media, or AI as primary sidebar items by default.
- Do not transform tabs, modals, overlays, filters, or states into standalone screens.
- Do not create a sitemap from every product capability.
